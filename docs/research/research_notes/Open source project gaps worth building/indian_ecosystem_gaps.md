# Open-source gaps specific to Indian developers and Indian digital public infrastructure (as of 22 October 2026)

Method note: repository stars, last push, archive status and licence were read directly from the GitHub REST API on 22 Oct 2026 (unauthenticated, then via `gh api`); npm figures from registry.npmjs.org and api.npmjs.org (downloads = last 30 days); PyPI from pypi.org JSON. "Last push" is the `pushed_at` field. Where a forum could not be reached from this environment it is listed under Gaps rather than guessed.

Status vocabulary used below: **exists and healthy** (maintained in 2026, non-trivial adoption), **exists but abandoned/thin** (archived, or no commits in 18+ months, or 0-3 stars with a single author), **does not exist** (no public repository found by GitHub search, npm search or web search).

## Key Question 1: DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025 — application toolkits beyond PII scanners

### Takeaway
As of October 2026 there are five or six young open-source DPDP consent/rights toolkits (the biggest has 14 stars), one WSO2-vendor accelerator, and a scattering of 0-star Spring Boot starters; none has adoption, none targets Next.js, Laravel or Express as middleware, and nothing implements consent-manager interoperability or a breach-notification workflow as a reusable library. Practitioners publicly state the ecosystem lacks tooling ("There's no ESLint plugin for this. No Semgrep rules. Nothing in the JS/TS ecosystem that understands Indian privacy law") and lacks a standard erasure interface between fiduciaries and consent managers.

### Cited Findings

Regulatory deadlines
- MeitY notified the DPDP Rules 2025 with phased commencement: Data Protection Board provisions immediately; "registration and obligations of consent managers, notice from data fiduciaries to individuals for processing their data and some other major norms related to processing of personal data will be implemented over a period of 12-18 months." — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/centre-notifies-dpdp-rules-implementation-planned-in-phases-spread-over-12-18-months-3798465); [The Week/PTI, 14 Nov 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/11/14/del148-biz-dpdp-rules-ld-govt.amp.html)
- Timeline as summarised by compliance consultancies: immediate (13 Nov 2025) Board constituted; 12 months (13 Nov 2026) consent-manager registration framework; 18 months, "a clear compliance deadline of May 13, 2027" for data fiduciary obligations. — [TCSA implementation roadmap](https://www.tcsa.in/resources/dpdp-rules-2025-implementation-roadmap); [Mondaq, DPDP Act compliance mandate](https://www.mondaq.com/india/privacy-protection/1708830/dpdp-act-compliance-mandate)
- Discrepancy: one Mondaq article states the Rules "were notified on 19 November 2025 in the official gazette" with 18-month operational obligations on 13 May 2027 — [Mondaq, A closer look at the DPDP Rules 2025](https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1706890/a-closer-look-at-the-dpdp-rules-2025). The 13 Nov 2025 date is the one used by PTI and most summaries; treat 19 Nov as an outlier.
- Breach notification: fiduciaries must inform affected individuals "without delay" and notify the Board with a first intimation "without delay", then a detailed report "within 72 hours" (extendable by the Board). — [Mondaq, A closer look](https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1706890/a-closer-look-at-the-dpdp-rules-2025)
- Retention/erasure: large e-commerce, gaming and social-media platforms above thresholds must "erase personal data after three years of user inactivity, with a 48-hour pre-deletion notice"; notices must give "an itemised description of the personal data to be processed" and be "presented independently of other information, in clear and plain language"; Significant Data Fiduciaries must do "a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) and an audit every 12 months". — [Mondaq, A closer look](https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1706890/a-closer-look-at-the-dpdp-rules-2025)
- Consent Manager regime: a Data Principal may "give, manage, review or withdraw consent across multiple Data Fiduciaries on a single interoperable platform"; registration requires incorporation in India and "a minimum net worth of ₹2 crore"; "Closing conditions require independent certification of the interoperable platform against the assurance framework the Board will publish from time to time"; consent managers "must maintain seven years of consent logs". — [Mondaq, Building the consent layer](https://www.mondaq.com/building-the-consent-layer-consent-managers-under-the-dpdp-rules-2025/1832140); [Tsaaro](https://tsaaro.com/blogs/consent-managers-under-the-dpdp-act-and-dpdp-rules-2025-functions-obligations-and-governance)
- Developer-facing requirement summary: "consent notices in 22 official languages", "data erasure mechanisms (within 30 days), and grievance redressal mechanisms (within 48 hours)" — [Vektorindex developer guide](https://www.vektorindex.com/compliance/india-dpdp-act-2025) (secondary; verify the 30-day/48-hour figures against the Gazette text before relying on them)

### Inferences
- The field is crowded with one-person prototypes created between June and October 2026 and empty of anything with users; a project that ships as installable middleware (Next.js route handlers/middleware, Laravel package, Spring Boot starter, Express) for the four data-principal rights plus retention jobs and a breach-report generator would have no incumbent.
- The consent-manager interoperability layer (13 Nov 2026 regime start; Board assurance framework still unpublished) is the one area where the only public artefacts are proposals (TSI's "Portable Consent Artifacts" and "Standardized Erasure Interface"), so a reference adapter plus a mock consent manager for local testing would be first-of-kind.
- The security-bug history in tsi-dpdp-cms (auth bypass, unauthenticated bootstrap, OTP brute force) is itself evidence that a security-focused maintainer has an edge here.

## Key Question 2: CERT-In directions (6-hour reporting, 180-day logs, NTP)

### Takeaway
The April 2022 directions remain in force unchanged in 2026, and July 2025 added SBOM v2.0 and annual third-party audit guidelines; open-source tooling for the 6-hour report, 180-day log retention evidence or NTP conformance consists of two 0-star individual/student projects from mid-2026 and nothing else, while every vendor (ManageEngine, Sumo Logic, Opsio) sells a commercial answer.

### Cited Findings
- Direction text (28 Apr 2022, effective "after 60 days from the date on which it is issued"): "(ii) Any service provider, intermediary, data centre, body corporate and Government organisation shall mandatorily report cyber incidents as mentioned in Annexure I to CERT-In within 6 hours of noticing such incidents or being brought to notice about such incidents. The incidents can be reported to CERT-In via email (incident@cert-in.org.in), Phone (1800-11-4949) and Fax (1800-11-6969)." — [CERT-In Directions PDF](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/CERT-In_Directions_70B_28.04.2022.pdf)
- "(iv) ... shall mandatorily enable logs of all their ICT systems and maintain them securely for a rolling period of 180 days and the same shall be maintained within the Indian jurisdiction." — same PDF
- "(i) ... shall connect to the Network Time Protocol (NTP) Server of National Informatics Centre (NIC) or National Physical Laboratory (NPL) or with NTP servers traceable to these NTP servers, for synchronisation of all their ICT systems clocks." — same PDF
- Annexure I lists 20 reportable incident types including "Targeted scanning/probing of critical networks/systems", "Data Breach", "Data Leak", "Attacks or incident affecting Digital Payment systems", "Attacks through Malicious mobile Apps", "Fake mobile Apps", "Unauthorised access to social media accounts". — same PDF
- CERT-In's directions page lists an extension issued 27 Jun 2022 (MSME timeline relief) and 2025-26 "NEW" guidance: "SBOM, QBOM & CBOM, AIBOM and HBOM Technical Guidelines (v2.0)", "15 Elemental Cyber Defense Controls for MSMEs", "Blueprint for AI-Assisted Vulnerability Exploitation Defense". — [CERT-In Directions70B page](https://www.cert-in.org.in/Directions70B.jsp)
- "On July 9, 2025, CERT-In released version 2 of the 'Technical Guidelines on SBOM, QBOM & CBOM, AIBOM, HBOM'" — [AZB Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/cert-ins-technical-guidelines-on-sbom-qbom-and-cbom-aibom-and-hbom/); "On July 25, 2025, CERT-In issued the Comprehensive Cyber Security Audit Policy Guidelines" — [Lawrbit](https://www.lawrbit.com/article/comprehensive-cyber-security-audit-policy-guidelines/)
- Vendor framing of the need: "If your firewall flags a brute-force attempt or your SIEM tool detects malware behaviour, the moment of detection is when the countdown begins." — [ManageEngine](https://www.manageengine.com/compliance-manager/cert-compliance/)
- GitHub search "cert-in incident reporting": 5 results. arpitgupta369/cert-in-log-analyzer — "Turn raw web and auth logs into ranked security findings and a ready-to-fill CERT-In 6-hour incident report"; author states it came from "my Young Scholar Traineeship with MeitY (CERT-In)"; 0 stars; MIT; created and last pushed 18 Jul 2026. lifeknife10A/barbarika — "Evidence-Preserving CERT-In Incident Readiness and Assisted Reporting Platform. SIH 2026"; "Pre-hackathon build phase"; 0 stars; no licence. — [cert-in-log-analyzer](https://github.com/arpitgupta369/cert-in-log-analyzer); [barbarika](https://github.com/lifeknife10A/barbarika)
- GitHub search "CERT-In compliance": 41 results, top ones are launch-readiness scanners (abdash1994/shipsafe-india, 2 stars; dhruvmagnate1122/beacon, 1 star) and RAG bots; none is a log-retention or NTP checker. — [search](https://github.com/search?q=CERT-In+compliance&type=repositories)
- A public readiness checklist exists as a Markdown file, not code. — [CyberSigma CERT-In Directions checklist](https://cybersigmacs.com/open/cert-in-directions-checklist.md)

### Inferences
- A small, well-tested kit (incident record schema matching CERT-In's reporting form, 6-hour timer with evidence log, a log-retention/NTP conformance checker for Linux/Docker/cloud) has no open-source competitor; the only prior art is a hackathon pre-build and a traineeship side project.
- Combining the kit with the DPDP 72-hour Board report (same incident, two regulators) would be distinctive.

### Gaps
- Could not quote the Annexure II reporting form fields (PDF text extraction returned headings only).
- No developer forum thread complaining specifically about CERT-In tooling was found; the demand evidence is the regulation itself plus vendor marketing.

## Key Question 3: India Stack / DPI — UPI, Account Aggregator, ONDC, ABDM, DigiLocker, Aadhaar offline eKYC, eSign, GSTN, BBPS

### Takeaway
Official open-source SDKs are essentially absent across the stack: Sahamati's spec repo has not been pushed since Dec 2022 and still carries "specs/aa.yaml is invalid" bugs from 2021, the only open FIU server (finarkein/fiul) is archived, ONDC's reference crypto utilities have unresolved "won't compile" and "BadPaddingException" issues from 2023-24, ABDM has only 0-3-star community SDKs, GSTN has one GPL Python IRN library, Aadhaar offline-XML verification has no JS/TS implementation, and eSign and BBPS have nothing.

## Candidate list: 8-15 open-source projects with a ready Indian audience (synthesis)

### Takeaway
